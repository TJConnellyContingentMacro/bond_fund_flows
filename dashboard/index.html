Method and caveats
  • Flow = (shares outstanding(t) − shares(t−1)) × NAV(t). NAV only, never market price.
  • Each fund's period runs between its own two most recent clean observations. Issuers publish on different lags, so periods end on different dates.
  • A period longer than three calendar days is flagged imputed and is excluded from z-score calibration.
  • Spread DV01 uses effective duration as a stand-in wherever a fund doesn't publish spread duration, which is most of them.
  • Totals are gross of fund-of-fund holdings. The overlay funds netted in the pipeline sit outside the core universe, and active multisector funds' ETF holdings are not netted.
