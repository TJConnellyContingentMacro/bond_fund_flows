Method and caveats
  • Flow = (shares outstanding(t) − shares(t−1)) × NAV(t). NAV only, never market price.
  • History before Sep 25 comes from iShares' and SSGA's own daily NAV and shares files; it matched the live pages exactly on every date checked.
  • The weekly series counts flows that span at most four calendar days (a weekend plus a holiday). Longer spans are catch-ups and are shown separately.
  • Issuers post on different lags, so the latest week can be partial for some of them.
  • Spread DV01 uses effective duration wherever a fund doesn't publish spread duration, which is most of them.
  • Totals are gross of fund-of-fund holdings. The option-overlay funds (TLTW, HYGW, LQDW) are outside the core universe.
